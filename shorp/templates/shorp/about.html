About Remise

Remise is a fashion destination for women and men who want clean, considered pieces without the noise. We work with a small set of makers to bring you well-cut essentials — dresses, tops, shoes and accessories — at honest prices.

This shorpfront was built as a final year software project, covering product browsing, a shopping bag, checkout, and secure payment.
